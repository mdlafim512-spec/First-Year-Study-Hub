1. Solanaceae (Nightshade Family)

The Solanaceae is one of the most economically important plant families in the world. It contains many food crops, medicinal plants, ornamental species and industrial crops. Members are rich in alkaloids and are distributed worldwide.

Habit

Mostly herbs, shrubs and small trees. Leaves are alternate and flowers are generally bisexual and actinomorphic.

Medicinal Value

Contains atropine, nicotine, capsaicin and other alkaloids used as sedatives, pain relievers and pharmaceuticals.

Economic Value

Provides potato, tomato, eggplant, chili and tobacco which are globally important agricultural products.

Additional Botanical Features

  • Floral formula: ⚥ K(5) C(5) A5 G̲(2)
  • Ovary superior and bicarpellary.
  • Fruit is usually a berry or capsule.
  • Seeds contain abundant endosperm.

2. Apocynaceae (Dogbane Family)

Apocynaceae includes many ornamental, medicinal and latex-producing plants. Most species possess milky sap and are widely cultivated in tropical regions.

Habit

Trees, shrubs, climbers and herbs containing characteristic latex.

Medicinal Value

Produces reserpine, vinblastine and vincristine used in hypertension and cancer treatment.

Economic Value

Important ornamental plants and sources of medicinal compounds.

3. Liliaceae (Lily Family)

Liliaceae contains some of the world's most beautiful ornamental flowering plants. Most members are perennial monocots with bulbs and showy flowers.

Habit

Perennial bulbous herbs with parallel venation and trimerous flowers.

Medicinal Value

Used traditionally for anti-inflammatory and skin-soothing purposes.

Economic Value

Major contributors to floriculture and landscaping industries.

4. Zingiberaceae (Ginger Family)

Zingiberaceae is one of the most economically valuable spice families. Members are aromatic plants possessing rhizomes rich in essential oils.

Habit

Perennial aromatic herbs with underground rhizomes and pseudostems.

Medicinal Value

Rich in gingerol, curcumin and essential oils used for digestive and anti-inflammatory treatments.

Economic Value

Source of ginger, turmeric, cardamom and valuable essential oils.

Interesting Facts

  • Turmeric contains curcumin, a powerful antioxidant.
  • Ginger helps reduce nausea and motion sickness.
  • Cardamom is known as the "Queen of Spices".
  • Zingiberaceae species are widely cultivated throughout Bangladesh.
